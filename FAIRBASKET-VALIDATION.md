The deadline is close, so keep this to a 15–20 minute discovery conversation. The goal is to validate the workflow, not to request money, sell software or claim a partnership without permission.

## Message to send in Portuguese

Olá! Estou desenvolvendo um protótipo gratuito chamado FairBasket para ajudar cozinhas comunitárias, associações e pequenas organizações a comparar compras essenciais e manter um registro claro das fontes de preço e das decisões.

Estou buscando conversar por 15 minutos com alguém que participe das compras ou da prestação de contas. Quero entender como vocês comparam ofertas hoje e quais comprovantes ajudam a explicar uma decisão de compra. Não estou pedindo doações nem preciso de nomes de beneficiários, dados bancários ou recibos com informações pessoais.

A conversa pode ser anônima; não vou citar o nome da organização nem usar suas respostas publicamente sem autorização explícita. Você teria disponibilidade para uma conversa curta nesta semana?

Obrigado pelo seu tempo!

## Short version in English

Hello! I am building a prototype called FairBasket to help small community organisations compare essential-goods offers and keep a clear record of price sources and buying decisions.

Could I ask someone involved in purchasing or accountability for a 15-minute conversation about how you compare offers today and what evidence is needed to explain a purchase? I am not asking for donations and do not need beneficiary details, bank information or unredacted receipts. I will not name the organisation or quote responses publicly without explicit permission.

Would someone be available for a short conversation this week? Thank you.

## Interview guide

Ask these questions; do not lead the participant toward praising the project.

1. What essential products do you buy repeatedly, and how frequently?
2. How do you compare prices today? Which details make two offers genuinely comparable: package size, brand/quality, seller, freight, delivery time, invoice, warranty?
3. What is the hardest part of explaining a purchase to a donor, board member or accountant?
4. How much time does a typical shopping cycle take, and what evidence do you keep?
5. What would make a comparison tool useful enough that your team would actually use it? What would make you distrust it?
6. Would you be willing to review a short prototype? If so, what information must never be public?

Record only the minimum notes needed for the prototype. Do not collect the names of beneficiaries, home addresses, account numbers, private donor records or copies of sensitive receipts.

## Evidence to retain for the hackathon

- Date of conversation and broad participant role, only with permission.
- A short anonymised summary of the pain point in the participant’s words, only if they approve being quoted.
- The current process and its rough time cost, described as self-reported rather than independently measured.
- A note of what remains unknown or where the participant disagrees with the proposed solution.
- Permission status for using any quote, logo or organisation name. Default: no public attribution.

## Pilot measures

Use a tiny baseline before claiming impact:

| Measure | Baseline | Prototype pilot |
|---|---|---|
| Time to document a basket comparison | Participant-reported minutes | Timed task with consent |
| Line items with an identified source | Count / total line items | Count / total line items |
| Like-for-like quote coverage | Count of comparable quotes | Count of comparable quotes |
| Evidence completeness | Source + timestamp + digest present? | Source + timestamp + digest present? |

Do not report percentage savings unless the compared items, quantities, conditions and freight are comparable and the organisation has verified the final purchase amount. A blockchain digest proves integrity of the recorded payload, not that the claim is true.

## What to say in the submission

Before a participant has tested it:
“FairBasket is designed to help small community buyers compare essential offers and preserve auditable evidence. Our next validation step is a consented pilot measuring comparison time, quote coverage and evidence completeness.”

After a participant has explicitly consented to a test, accurately describe the number of participants and what they tested. Do not imply formal adoption, endorsement or realised savings unless that is documented.
